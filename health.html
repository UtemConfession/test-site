UTeM Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU) Student Healthcare Master Guide

Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU UTeM) provides primary healthcare, emergency medical response, dispensary pharmacy services, dental treatments, first aid equipment loans, and confidential mental health counseling for all registered diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate students across Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) and Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh).

🏥 Available Medical Services & Clinic Hours

  • Outpatient Medical Consultation (Pesakit Luar): General diagnosis, fever/flu treatments, chronic disease monitoring, and minor surgical procedures (dressing & wound care). Operating Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
  • Dispensary Pharmacy: Dispensing prescribed generic medications and medical supplies covered under student health fees.
  • Dental Clinic (Klinik Pergigian): Routine oral check-ups, scaling, fillings, and tooth extractions by appointment via the PKU dental portal.
  • First-Aid Kits Loan: Equipment supply for approved student association events and outdoor sports tournaments.

🚨 24/7 Emergency Response & Ambulance Hotline

For acute medical emergencies, vehicle collisions, or severe injuries occurring outside regular clinic hours:

  1. Call the UTeM Security Control Room (Pejabat Keselamatan) 24/7 Hotline: 06-270 1200 or 06-270 1300.
  2. State the exact casualty location (e.g. Kolej Kediaman Satria Block B, FTMK Level 2, or Lestari Cafe).
  3. The control room will immediately dispatch the on-duty campus ambulance or coordinate with Hospital Melaka / Hospital Alor Gajah for emergency room triage.

📄 Medical Certificate (MC) Endorsement Rules

If you receive a Medical Certificate (MC / Cuti Sakit) from an external private clinic or government hospital, university academic regulations require you to submit the physical slip to PKU for endorsement by a UTeM Medical Officer within 48 hours. Unverified external MCs may not be accepted by your faculty for missed midterms, quizzes, or final examinations.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I get private clinic MCs validated by PKU for missed exams? Medical certificates (MC) issued by external private clinics must be presented in person to PKU within 48 hours of issue for official endorsement by a UTeM Medical Officer before submission to your faculty dean's office.
Are medical consultations and dispensary medications free for UTeM students? Yes, basic primary outpatient consultations, standard dispensary medications, and minor treatments at PKU are covered by student health service fees included in your semester registration.
How do I request ambulance transport for after-hours on-campus emergencies? Contact the 24/7 UTeM Security Control Centre (Pejabat Keselamatan) immediately at 06-270 1200 or 06-270 1300 to dispatch the on-duty campus emergency response vehicle or coordinate with 999.
