UTeM Undergraduate Scholarship & Financial Assistance Directory

Financing tertiary education at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) is supported by a robust ecosystem of government education loans, federal talent sponsorships, state education foundations, and emergency campus zakat welfare funds. Understanding eligibility criteria, income thresholds, and application timelines ensures students secure adequate funding throughout their degree or diploma journey.

💰 Primary Financial Aid Categories

  • PTPTN Higher Education Loan: The universal loan covering tuition fees and living expenses, disbursed on a three-tier income scale: B40 (Maximum up to RM6,800/yr for science/tech degrees), M40 (75% tier), and T20 (50% tier). Eligible for 100% complete waiver upon achieving First Class Honours.
  • MARA TESP (Tertiary Education Sponsorship Programme): Convertible study loan for Bumiputera students pursuing engineering, computing, and technology degrees with competitive conversion rates based on final graduating CGPA.
  • JPA Federal Scholarships (PIDN / LSPN): Prestigious direct sponsorships managed via the JPA eSILA portal for top academic performers maintaining CGPA ≥ 3.75.
  • State Education Foundations (Yayasan Negeri): State-based funding bodies including TAPEM Melaka, Yayasan Johor (YPJ), Yayasan Negeri Sembilan (YNS), Yayasan Selangor, Yayasan Sarawak (YSSP), and Yayasan Pahang providing dermasiswa and study loans for state-born scholars.
  • UTeM Pusat Islam & Zakat Aid: Emergency subsistence support, laptop purchase grants, and food aid for asnaf and B40 students via Bahagian Hal Ehwal Pelajar (BHEP).

📋 Application Documentation Checklist

  1. Offer Letter (Surat Tawaran): Official UTeM admission offer letter downloaded via the admission portal.
  2. Certified Income Proof: Latest salary slips (Penyata Gaji), EA/EC forms, or official Village Head/JKKK income declaration forms for self-employed parents.
  3. Academic Transcripts: SPM / STPM / Matriculation / Diploma certificates certified as True Copies (CTC) by an authorized Grade A government officer.
  4. Bank Account Verification: Active individual savings account under the student's name (e.g. Bank Islam for PTPTN disbursements).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I hold both a state foundation grant and a PTPTN loan simultaneously? Most state foundations (like TAPEM or Yayasan Johor) allow one-off subsistence dermasiswa grants alongside PTPTN. However, major federal scholarships like JPA or MARA prohibit concurrent active PTPTN loans.
How do I apply for the PTPTN First Class Loan Repayment Exemption at UTeM? Graduates achieving First Class Honours (CGPA ≥ 3.70/3.75) must submit an exemption application via the myPTPTN portal within 12 months of their official UTeM Convocation Ceremony along with certified degree certificates.
What is the minimum CGPA required to keep PTPTN loan disbursements active? PTPTN requires students to maintain a minimum GPA of 2.00 (Kedudukan Baik) every semester. If your GPA drops below 2.00, PTPTN disbursements will be suspended until your academic standing recovers.
