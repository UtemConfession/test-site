Posting Guidelines

  • Anonymous: Your IP address, name, or metadata are not tracked or saved.
  • Respect: No target bullying, sexual harassment, or hate speech against students or lecturers.
  • Spam: Submit button has a 30-second delay between posts.
  • Images: Do not upload NSFW content, private images of individuals, or screenshots of social media posts containing visible faces or personal information.
  • Moderation: Image submissions are read and reviewed by human moderators before being published.
  • Links: Links can only be shared in image form. This is so that the admin can review first before sharing to the channel.
  • Impersonation: Don't pretend to be another student, lecturer, staff member, or organization in a way that could mislead others.
  • Threats: Threats of violence, intimidation, or encouragement of harm are not allowed.
  • Accusations: Do not post unproven allegations about identifiable people. Keep discussions factual and anonymous.
  • Connections: Posts seeking friendship, companionship, study partners, or other forms of connections are required to be respectful of boundaries, moral standards and responses.
  • Think Before You Post: Once published, a confession may be seen, shared, or screenshotted by others.
Community Moderation & Takedown Notice Submissions are actively moderated for profanity, defamation, and personal data in compliance with PDPA 2010. UTeM Confessions Pro Max reserves the right to reject or remove content that violates these policies. To report abusive content or request a takedown, contact us immediately at utemconfessionpromax@duck.com.

Telegram Terms & Channel Safety

🛡️ Protecting Our Student Channel Telegram actively terminates channels that violate its terms or receive mass abuse reports. Help us keep UCPM online by adhering to these safety standards:
🚨 Activities That Can Get the Channel Banned:
  • ✕ Mass-Reportable Doxxing: Sharing private phone numbers, IC numbers, matric IDs, residential room addresses, or unconsented photos triggers Telegram report cascades that cause automated permanent channel bans.
  • ✕ DMCA & Copyright Strikes: Distributing commercial e-textbooks, paywalled test-banks, cracked software, or leaked official exam papers. Telegram strictly complies with automated copyright take-downs.
  • ✕ Spam, Bot Links & Scams: Shortened links (bit.ly), MLM/get-rich-quick promotions, crypto spam, or unsolicited channel invite links get caught in Telegram's automated spam filters.
  • ✕ Drugs & Illegal Substances: Zero tolerance for narcotics, cannabis, ketum, illicit vape pods (e.g., mushroom/magic vape), or prescription drug sales. Violates Telegram TOS, Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, and UTeM Disciplinary Code.
  • ✕ Explicit or Non-Consensual Media: Any NSFW content, suggestive media taken without consent, or revenge material is strictly prohibited under Telegram TOS and Section 233 of the Malaysian CMA 1998.
🔐 Using Services with Google:
  • ✦ Google Sign-In Privacy: Google OAuth is used solely for anti-bot verification. Your Google name, email address, and profile are never sent to Telegram or publicly revealed.
  • ✦ Google Drive Links: When sharing study notes via Google Drive, set permissions to 'Anyone with the link can view'. Ensure no executable (.exe/.apk) files or third-party personal data are included.
  • ✦ Student Email Safety: Avoid using your official @student.utem.edu.my account for public file distribution, as institutional Google Workspace domains are monitored under university IT policies.
