UTeM Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU) Student Health Guide

Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU UTeM) provides primary healthcare, emergency response dispatch, dental treatments, first aid equipment loans, and confidential mental health counseling to undergraduates at Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) and Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh). For after-hours emergencies, contact the 24/7 Pejabat Keselamatan UTeM hotline.
