UTeM IT Setup: Free Microsoft 365, MATLAB, Eduroam & Student Email Guide

🕒 8 Min Read 💻 Digital Perks & Software Licenses 🎓 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
Peer-Reviewed & Fact-Checked Guide Authored by UTeM Senior Undergraduates & verified against official university academic handbooks.
Fact-Checked: August 2026 • Editorial Standards ➔

As a student at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM), your official student matric email (@student.utem.edu.my) unlocks thousands of Ringgit worth of enterprise software licenses, cloud storage, high-speed worldwide Wi-Fi, and academic tools provided by the Pusat Teknologi Maklumat (PTM).

Many undergraduates are unaware of these free perks. This guide shows you how to claim your Microsoft Office 365 subscription, download engineering suites like MATLAB and CAD, and configure rock-solid Eduroam Wi-Fi across all your devices.

1. Claiming Free Microsoft 365 & 1TB OneDrive Storage

Every active UTeM student is entitled to install authentic Microsoft 365 Apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Teams) on up to 5 PCs/Macs, 5 tablets, and 5 smartphones:

  1. Visit portal.office.com.
  2. Log in using your full UTeM student email format: B03XXXXXXXX@student.utem.edu.my (replace with your matric number).
  3. Enter your UTeM Single Sign-On (SSO) password.
  4. Click the "Install apps" button at the top-right corner to download the offline installer.

2. Free Engineering & Development Software Licenses

Software Package Target Disciplines How to Access / License Portal
MATLAB & Simulink FKE, FKM, FTTK, FKP (All Engineering) Create a MathWorks account using your UTeM email to download the Campus-Wide License.

3. Connecting to Eduroam Wi-Fi (No Authentication Loops)

Eduroam allows UTeM students to securely connect to high-speed internet at UTeM campuses as well as thousands of universities worldwide using their UTeM credentials:

  • SSID Network Name: eduroam
  • Identity / Username: YourMatricNumber@student.utem.edu.my
  • Password: Your UTeM Student Portal password
  • EAP Method: PEAP
  • Phase 2 Authentication: MSCHAPV2
  • CA Certificate: Select "Don't validate" or "Use system certificates" (Domain: utem.edu.my)

For more network configurations and diagnostic portals, check our Wi-Fi & Essential Links Hub.

4. IT Support & PTM Helpdesk

If you encounter account lockouts, Wi-Fi MAC address blocking, or portal login issues, reach out to the university IT desk:

  • Helpdesk Portal: Pusat Teknologi Maklumat (PTM), Level 1, Bangunan PTM, Kampus Induk.
  • Email Support: helpdesk@utem.edu.my
💡 Backup Pro-Tip: Set up Microsoft OneDrive auto-sync on your laptop for your assignment and FYP folders. Your 1TB cloud storage ensures your code and documents are safe even if your hardware suffers a sudden crash.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do my Microsoft 365 and OneDrive accounts expire after graduation? Student licenses remain active for approximately 3 to 6 months post-convocation, after which accounts transition to alumni status. Always back up important thesis files to personal hard drives before graduating.
Why does Eduroam say 'Authentication Problem'? Ensure you include the full domain suffix @student.utem.edu.my in your username. Entering only your matric number will cause the RADIUS server to reject the handshake.
