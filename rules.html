UTeM Confessions Community Guidelines & Rules

UTeM Confessions Pro Max (UCPM) is dedicated to fostering an open, engaging, and supportive platform for the students and campus community of Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). To ensure this space remains safe, respectful, and legally compliant, all users must adhere to the following rules before submitting content.

1. Core Community Standards

🚫 1. Zero Bullying, Defamation & Hate Speech

Targeted harassment, naming-and-shaming, malicious rumours, cyberbullying, or discriminatory remarks based on race, religion, gender, or disability are strictly forbidden. Posts targeting individuals with abusive intent will be rejected immediately.

🔒 2. Privacy Protection & No Doxxing

Do not disclose personally identifiable information (PII) including student matric numbers, full IC numbers, private phone numbers, vehicle license plates, personal social media handles, or residential hostel room numbers without written authorization.

📸 3. Image & Media Submission Standards

All uploaded images undergo human pre-moderation. Submissions must not contain NSFW/explicit content, unconsented photos of individuals in private spaces, or unblurred screenshots of private messaging apps.

⚖️ 4. Academic Integrity & Legal Compliance

Content promoting academic dishonesty (e.g., paid assignment completion, FYP ghostwriting rings, exam leakage), illegal gambling, substance abuse, or unauthorized political rallies is prohibited and violates the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 (CMA 1998).

📢 5. Commercial Spam & Advertising Policy

The confession board is not a classified ads billboard. Affiliate links, pyramid/MLM schemes, repetitive commercial spam, and bot-generated promotions will be blocked. Legitimate student services can be listed under the Marketplace Tab.

🤝 6. Constructive Campus Discussion

Constructive feedback regarding campus facilities, library services, Wi-Fi connectivity, or cafeteria food quality is welcomed when framed factually and respectfully. Critique the issue, not the person.

2. Moderation Workflow & Rate Limiting

Submissions are queued securely through encrypted APIs and screened by automated filtering algorithms and volunteer student moderators. A mandatory 30-second submission cooldown prevents spam flooding. Submission of a confession does not guarantee automated publishing.

3. Notice & Takedown Policy (Content Removal)

If a published confession inadvertently identifies you, infringes on your privacy, or causes personal distress, you can request an expedited takedown. Contact our admin team via our Telegram channel (@utemconf) or email utemconfessionpromax@duck.com with the post number and removal rationale. Verified requests are processed within 24 hours.

💡 Think Before You Post: Anonymity provides freedom of expression, but ethical responsibility rests with the author. Uphold the reputation and harmony of our university community.
