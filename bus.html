📍 Important Student Fare & Route Details:
Bus M10A (Pink Bus) connects Melaka Sentral Bus Terminal (Platform / Bay 17) directly to UTeM Kampus Induk (Main Gate / FTMK Terminal) via Batu Berendam Free Trade Zone, Ayer Keroh, MITC, and Durian Tunggal.
• Service Days: Operates Friday, Saturday, and Sunday extending directly into UTeM Kampus Induk.
• Fare: Approx RM 2.50 – RM 3.00 between Melaka Sentral and UTeM Kampus Induk.
• Payment Modality: 100% Cashless via Touch 'n Go (minimum RM5 balance), Contactless MyDebit Card, DuitNow QR, or MyRapid Concession Pass. Cash is strictly not accepted on board.

📋 BAS.MY M10A Operations & Transit Guidelines:

⭐ Weekend Direct Campus Entry (Fri – Sun): On Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, Route M10A runs directly between Melaka Sentral and UTeM Kampus Induk Terminal (FTMK Bus Stop) for direct outstation travel.
💳 100% Cashless Payment System: Payment onboard is strictly cashless. Passengers can tap a Touch 'n Go card (min RM5 balance), MyDebit Contactless card, DuitNow QR, or use a MyRapid concession pass. No cash accepted.
📱 Live Transit GPS Tracking: Track real-time bus arrivals, vehicle GPS locations, and delay alerts by downloading the LUGO App, Moovit, or Pulse by Prasarana and selecting Melaka Route M10A.

UTeM Shuttle Bus Transit Guide & BAS.MY Public Network Master Guide

Navigating transit between Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) campuses requires understanding both the complimentary internal university shuttle bus fleet and the public State transit network operated by BAS.MY Melaka (Route M10A). With faculties distributed across two distinct geographical zones—Kampus Induk in Durian Tunggal and Kampus Teknologi in Ayer Keroh separated by 12km via Lebuhraya Ayer Keroh—punctual transit planning is vital for arriving on time for lectures, laboratories, and examination halls.

💖 BAS.MY M10A Pink Bus Public Transit Guide

The state-managed pink bus fleet provides essential public transit connecting Melaka Sentral transit terminal directly to UTeM and surrounding Ayer Keroh commercial hubs:

🚍 Weekday Operations (Mon – Thu)

Operates between Melaka Sentral ⇄ Batu Berendam ⇄ MITC / Mydin Ayer Keroh from 06:00 to 20:00. Students disembarking at MITC can connect to university shuttle lines.

⭐ Weekend Direct Campus Entry (Fri – Sun)

On Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, Route M10A extends past MITC to Zoo Melaka and drives directly into the UTeM Kampus Induk main terminal, providing direct access for outstation travelers.

💳 100% Cashless Transit Payments

BAS.MY accepts Touch 'n Go cards (min RM5 balance), MyRapid student concession cards, contactless MyDebit cards, and DuitNow QR. Cash is strictly not accepted on board.

💡 Essential Commuter Strategies & Transit Etiquette

1. Lebuhraya Ayer Keroh Peak Hour Buffers Allow a minimum of 25–35 minutes for transit between Kampus Induk and Kampus Teknologi during morning peak hours (07:45–08:30) and evening return hours (16:45–17:30) due to traffic at the MITC / Ayer Keroh toll interchange.
2. Final Exam Week Deployment During official university examination weeks, the Transport Unit deploys high-capacity double-trip shuttles starting 45 minutes prior to each morning (09:00) and afternoon (14:30) exam slot. Arrive at designated stops 15 minutes early.
3. E-Hailing & Grab Fare-Splitting Pickup Points Popular student carpool and Grab ride-sharing hubs are located at Pintu Gerbang Utama Kampus Induk, FTMK Lobby, and the Ayer Keroh MITC Mydin bus bay (averaging RM10–RM14 per car trip).
4. UTeM Transport Unit Hotline & Lost Property If you accidentally leave your student matric card, laptop, or bag on an internal bus, contact the UTeM Transport Unit Hotline at 011-11494251 or visit the Unit Pengangkutan counter next to Bengkel Utama Kampus Induk.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the internal UTeM campus shuttle bus free for students? Yes, all official internal UTeM campus shuttle buses operating between Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal), Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh), Kolej Kediaman Satria, and Kolej Kediaman Lestari are completely free of charge for all enrolled students.
How does the BAS.MY M10A pink bus service connect to UTeM? The public BAS.MY M10A pink bus runs from Melaka Sentral to MITC. On Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, the route extends directly to Zoo Melaka and the UTeM Main Campus main entrance terminal.
What payment methods are accepted on the BAS.MY M10A pink bus? BAS.MY operates strictly cashless. Passengers can pay using a Touch 'n Go card, MyRapid transit pass, contactless debit card, or DuitNow QR scanner on board.
Do campus shuttle buses operate during semester breaks? During semester holidays and inter-semester study breaks, internal shuttle frequencies are scaled down to reduced skeleton services operating between KI and KT for postgraduate and administrative needs.
How can I track real-time bus arrivals in Melaka? You can track public BAS.MY buses live using the official Pulse by Prasarana or Moovit mobile application by selecting the Melaka public transport network.
Can I bring bicycles, electric scooters, or heavy luggage on board? Foldable electric scooters and luggage are permitted on internal shuttles provided they do not obstruct walkways during peak crowding. Full-size bicycles must use outdoor bike racks.

Campus Transit & Bus Route Guide

Navigating the UTeM campus bus system is crucial for arriving at lectures on time. The university operates multiple routes connecting Kampus Induk (Main Campus) and Kampus Teknologi (City Campus) with various residential colleges (Kolej Kediaman).

  • Peak Hours: Buses are most crowded between 7:30 AM - 8:30 AM and 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM. Arrive at the bus stop at least 15 minutes early during these times.
  • Kampus Induk Routes: Covers FTMK, FKE, FKM, Library, and main administrative buildings.
  • Kampus Teknologi Routes: Connects city campus students. Always check the real-time schedule as traffic conditions may cause delays.
