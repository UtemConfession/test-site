UTeM GPA & CGPA Calculation Scale Guide

Calculate semester GPA (Grade Point Average) and cumulative CGPA for Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka undergraduates. UTeM uses the 4.00 quality point system where A = 4.00, A- = 3.70, B+ = 3.30, B = 3.00, B- = 2.70, C+ = 2.30, C = 2.00, C- = 1.70, D+ = 1.30, D = 1.00, F = 0.00. Students achieving a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher earn Anugerah Dekan (Dean's List) recognition.
