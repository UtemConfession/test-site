UTeM Campus Wi-Fi Credentials & Official Portal Directory

Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) provides multiple wireless network options for students and staff across all campuses. The primary networks include Wifi UTeM Net (shared password), UTeM-Wifi (student ID-based PEAP/MSCHAPv2 authentication), and eduroam (global academic roaming with @student.utem.edu.my credentials). For off-campus access to library databases and e-journals, students can use SoftEther VPN or register a LibProxy account. This page also links to the official UTeM Helpdesk (ServiceDesk), Staff Directory, MPP student council channels, MOHE e-Aduan complaints portal, convocation resources, and the student vehicle application form.
