CAREER & INDUSTRIAL TRAINING

UTeM Industrial Training (Latihan Industri) Guide: Timeline, Placements & Assessment

🕒 9 Min Read 💼 Career & Practical Training 🎓 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
Peer-Reviewed & Fact-Checked Guide Authored by UTeM Senior Undergraduates & verified against official university academic handbooks.
Fact-Checked: August 2026 • Editorial Standards ➔

Industrial Training (Latihan Industri / LI) is a mandatory graduation requirement for all Bachelor's Degree and Diploma undergraduates at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). As a technical university focused on application-driven engineering, IT, and technology management, your internship is where theory meets high-stakes industrial practice.

Securing an impactful internship placement requires strategic planning months before your training semester begins. This comprehensive guide details the complete UTeM LI process from initial application to final supervisor evaluations.

1. The UTeM Industrial Training Timeline

Most faculties (FTMK, FKE, FKM, FKP, FTTK, FPTT) schedule Industrial Training during the final semester or between Year 3 and Year 4 (duration: 10 to 24 weeks depending on the program). Here is the recommended preparation roadmap:

Phase & Timing Key Action Items Responsible Entity
Phase 1: 5–6 Months Prior Attend faculty LI briefing. Request official Cover Letter & Confirmation of Student Status via the UTeM LI Portal. Faculty LI Coordinator / Student
Phase 2: 3–4 Months Prior Send 15–30 tailored resumes to targeted companies. Follow up by phone/email. Secure Offer Letter. Student / Hiring Company
Phase 3: 1 Month Prior Submit company acceptance letter (Surat Jawapan Penerimaan) to Faculty Coordinator for syllabus suitability approval. Faculty Committee
Phase 4: Training Period Maintain daily logbook entries, record technical milestones, and host Academic Visiting Lecturer (Penyelia Lawatan). Company Mentor & Student

2. Top Industrial Placement Hubs for UTeM Students

UTeM undergraduates are widely recognized across Malaysian industrial corridors. Depending on your faculty, here are key industrial zones frequented by alumni:

  • Melaka Free Trade Zone (FTZ) & Industrial Parks: Batu Berendam (Infineon Technologies, Texas Instruments, Dominant Opto Technologies), Melaka World Solar Valley, and CTRM Aero Composites.
  • Klang Valley / Selangor: Technology Park Malaysia (TPM), Cyberjaya Tech Hubs, Subang Aerospace Park, and Shah Alam manufacturing plants.
  • Northern Tech Corridor (Penang & Kulim): Intel, Micron, Keysight, Osram, and Western Digital.
  • Southern Corridor (Johor Bahru & Pasir Gudang): Pengerang Integrated Complex, Senai Hi-Tech Park, and Medini Iskandar.

3. Internship Allowances & Budgeting

While university regulations prioritize learning objectives, most reputable engineering and tech firms provide monthly student stipends. In Malaysia, standard technical internship allowances typically range from RM600 to RM1,500+ per month. If interning outside Melaka, budget carefully for room rental, meals, and transport.

4. Logbook & Final Grading Criteria

Industrial Training is graded on a Pass/Fail or graded credit basis depending on your curriculum structure. Evaluation consists of three primary components:

  1. Industry Supervisor Evaluation (40%–50%): Punctuality, teamwork, technical problem-solving, and professional conduct.
  2. Visiting Academic Supervisor Assessment (20%–30%): Review of logbook quality, workplace demonstration, and oral interview during the site visit.
  3. Final Technical Report & Logbook (20%–30%): Comprehensive documentation of engineering/IT projects completed during your tenure.
💡 Pro-Tip for UTeM Students: Always update your e-Logbook weekly rather than leaving it to the final month. High-quality logbook reflections with diagrams and code snippets make an exceptional impression during your academic supervisor's visit.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I do my internship at a startup or remote company? Yes, provided the company has a registered business license (SSM) and can assign a qualified full-time mentor in your study discipline for supervision.
What happens if I receive multiple offer letters? Politely decline unwanted offers in writing within 5 business days. Never accept multiple offers formally, as withdrawing after signing damages UTeM's institutional relationship with employers.
