                    🚨 "Delivered But Not Received?" Quick Action Checklist
                

                

                    If your tracking app says "Delivered" but the item isn't in your hands yet, do not panic. Follow these four steps:
                


                

                    

                        1
                        

                            Allow 2–4 Hours for Unit Mel Sorting: Couriers often bulk-scan all campus packages as "Delivered" when they unload boxes at the Student Centre. Unit Mel staff need time to sort and log them.
                        

                    

                    

                        2
                        

                            Check the Proof of Delivery (POD) Photo: Open the courier tracking page or Shopee/Lazada app. Look at the POD signature photo to see which counter or rack your box was placed on.
                        

                    

                    

                        3
                        

                            Call the Dispatch Driver Directly: In the tracking status, tap the driver icon to call the assigned delivery courier directly while they are still in the Hang Tuah Jaya / Durian Tunggal area.
                        

                    

                    

                        4
                        

                            Visit Unit Mel with Tracking ID: If still missing after 24 hours, visit the Unit Mel counter at the Student Centre with your tracking number and IC to check the master incoming delivery manifest.
