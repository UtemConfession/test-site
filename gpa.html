UTeM GPA & CGPA Calculation Guide & Academic Grading Scale

Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) employs the standard Malaysian 4.00 Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system across all undergraduate, diploma, and postgraduate faculties. Understanding how quality points and credit hours intersect is essential for maintaining good academic standing (Kedudukan Baik), targeting Dean's List (Anugerah Dekan), and qualifying for First Class PTPTN loan exemptions.

📐 How to Calculate UTeM GPA & CGPA Step-by-Step

GPA is computed by dividing your Total Quality Points (Jumlah Nilai Mata) by your Total Registered Credit Hours (Jumlah Jam Kredit) for the semester:

GPA = Total (Credit Hours × Grade Point Value) ÷ Total Semester Credit Hours
CGPA = Cumulative (All Quality Points Across All Semesters) ÷ Cumulative Total Earned Credit Hours

Example Calculation: If you take 3 credits of Software Engineering (Grade A, 4.00 = 12.00 points), 4 credits of Data Structures (Grade B+, 3.30 = 13.20 points), and 3 credits of Calculus (Grade B, 3.00 = 9.00 points), your Total Quality Points are 34.20 divided by 10 credits = GPA 3.42.

⚖️ UTeM Academic Standing (Status Akademik) Rules

🟢 Kedudukan Baik (KB - Good Standing) Cumulative CGPA ≥ 2.00. Students maintain unrestricted course registration privileges and can register full-time credit loads (12 to 20 credit hours).
🟡 Kedudukan Bersyarat (KS - Academic Probation) Cumulative CGPA between 1.70 and 1.99. Maximum credit load is strictly restricted to 12 credit hours to facilitate academic recovery. PTPTN loan disbursements are suspended while on KS.
🔴 Kedudukan Gagal & Diberhentikan (KG - Academic Dismissal) Cumulative CGPA < 1.70 in any single semester, or receiving KS for two consecutive regular semesters. Students dismissed under KG may submit an official appeal to the university Senate within 14 days of result release.

🏆 Anugerah Dekan & First Class Honours Criteria

To be awarded the prestigious Anugerah Dekan (Dean's List) certificate at UTeM, a student must fulfill all three criteria in a single regular academic semester:

  1. Achieve a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher.
  2. Register and complete a minimum of 12 graded credit hours (excluding non-graded audit or Pass/Fail courses like Kokurikulum).
  3. Possess no failing grades (F/E) or incomplete grades (TL) in that semester.
🎓 PTPTN Loan-to-Scholarship Conversion (First Class) Graduates completing their bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (CGPA ≥ 3.70) can apply for 100% PTPTN loan exemption through the myPTPTN portal within 12 months of official graduation.

🚀 Grade Improvement & Course Retake Strategy (Ulang Gred)

Under UTeM academic regulations, students can redeem low grades by repeating courses in subsequent semesters:

  • Grade Replacement Impact: When you repeat a course with a previous grade of C-, D, or F, the newer grade point value replaces the older one in cumulative CGPA computations.
  • High-Credit Course Priority: Prioritize repeating core 3-credit and 4-credit courses. Replacing an F (0.00) in a 4-credit course with an A- (3.70) yields a massive +14.80 quality point boost.
  • Transcript Notation: Both attempts remain logged on your permanent transcript, but only the latest/higher grade contributes to the graduating CGPA.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does repeating a failed course affect my UTeM CGPA? When you repeat a course, the new grade replaces the previous failing grade (Grade F) in the cumulative CGPA calculation, significantly raising your CGPA. However, the original attempt remains visible on your official academic transcript.
Does PTPTN freeze if my GPA drops below 2.00? Yes. PTPTN requires all loan recipients to maintain Kedudukan Baik (GPA ≥ 2.00). If your GPA falls below 2.00, PTPTN disbursements will be suspended for the following semester until your standing recovers.
What CGPA is required for First Class degree honours at UTeM? First Class Honours (Ijazah Kelas Pertama) generally requires a graduating CGPA of 3.70 and above (or 3.50+ depending on specific engineering accreditation faculty guidelines). First-class graduates are eligible for 100% PTPTN loan repayment exemptions.
Do Pass/Fail (TL/HL) courses count toward my semester GPA? No. Non-graded courses such as Kokurikulum (HL/TL) or Credit Transfers award credits toward degree completion but are omitted from GPA and CGPA quality point calculations.
How can I recover from Kedudukan Bersyarat (KS) probation? To clear KS, you must achieve a semester GPA high enough to pull your cumulative CGPA back to ≥ 2.00. Focus intensely on repeating failed subjects and managing the restricted 12-credit workload.
Can I appeal if I receive Kedudukan Gagal (KG)? Yes. Students can submit an official Rayuan Meneruskan Pengajian (Appeal to Continue Studies) to the Academic Management Division (BPA) within 14 working days of official result announcement.

UTeM 4.0 Grading System Guide

Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) uses a standard 4.0 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system. Understanding how your grades are calculated is essential for maintaining your academic standing.

  • Dean's List (Anugerah Dekan): Achieving a Grade Point Average (GPA / PNG) of 3.50 and above in a regular semester with a minimum of 12 credit hours.
  • Academic Probation (Kedudukan Bersyarat): A CGPA (PNGK) below 2.00 but above 1.70 places you on academic probation. You must raise your CGPA in the following semester.
  • Dismissal (Gagal Diberhentikan): A CGPA below 1.70, or failing to raise your CGPA above 2.00 after being on probation, may result in academic dismissal.
