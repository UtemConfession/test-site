UTeM Final Year Project (FYP / PSM) Survival Roadmap

🕒 10 Min Read 🔬 Capstone Research & Defense 🎓 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
Peer-Reviewed & Fact-Checked Guide Authored by UTeM Senior Undergraduates & verified against official university academic handbooks.
Fact-Checked: August 2026 • Editorial Standards ➔

The Projek Sarjana Muda (PSM / FYP) is the capstone pinnacle of your undergraduate journey at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). Spanning two consecutive semesters (PSM 1 and PSM 2), it tests your technical competence, project management, prototyping ability, and academic research rigor.

Whether you are building an IoT sensor network at FKE, designing a robotics mechanism at FKM, training a deep learning computer vision model at FTMK, or optimizing manufacturing workflows at FKP, this roadmap guides you through every crucial milestone.

1. Understanding PSM 1 vs. PSM 2 Requirements

Deliverable Feature PSM 1 (Proposal & Preliminary Design) PSM 2 (Implementation & Final Defense)
Primary Objective Problem statement formulation, literature review, methodology planning, and initial simulations/proof-of-concept. Full hardware fabrication, software integration, experimental testing, data benchmarking, and commercial viability.
Key Milestone Proposal Defense (Pembentangan Cadangan) & Chapter 1–3 Draft. Final Viva Voce / Poster Presentation & Complete 5-Chapter Bound Thesis.
Weightage on CGPA Typically 2–4 Credit Hours (Foundational). Typically 4–6 Credit Hours (Direct impact on Honours classification).

2. Choosing the Right Supervisor (SV)

Your relationship with your Supervisor will define your final year experience. Consider these practical strategies when selecting a mentor:

  • Match Research Specialization: Check your faculty directory for lecturers actively publishing in your target subfield (e.g., Embedded Systems, AI, Renewable Energy, Polymer Composites).
  • Grant & Lab Resources: Supervisors with active FRGS or industrial research grants often have dedicated laboratory benches, testing equipment, and funds to sponsor component purchases.
  • Supervisory Style: Clarify expectations early on whether your supervisor prefers weekly face-to-face progress check-ins or milestone-driven asynchronous updates.

3. Hardware Sourcing & Prototyping in Melaka

Engineering and IoT students frequently need microcontrollers, sensors, 3D printing filaments, and mechanical parts. Common sourcing channels include:

  • Online Fast-Dispatch Vendors: Cytron Technologies, element14, RS Components, and Shopee/Lazada local electronic specialists.
  • On-Campus Workshops: Utilize faculty maker-spaces at FTTK, FKM Central Workshop, and FTMK Fab Labs for PCB milling, laser cutting, and 3D printing.
  • UTeM Library E-Databases: Leverage IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and Scopus through the Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) Portal to extract benchmarking methodologies.

4. UTeM Thesis Formatting & Plagiarism Checks

Ensure your final manuscript adheres to the standard Panduan Penulisan Tesis UTeM. Essential criteria to monitor:

  1. Turnitin Plagiarism Index: Most faculties enforce a similarity index threshold below 20% to 25% (excluding bibliography and quoted definitions).
  2. Chapter Hierarchy: Chapter 1 (Introduction), Chapter 2 (Literature Review), Chapter 3 (Methodology), Chapter 4 (Results & Discussion), Chapter 5 (Conclusion & Recommendations).
  3. Reference Standards: Use reference management tools like Mendeley or Zotero (IEEE style for engineering/IT; APA style for technology management).
💡 Defense Day Pro-Tip: Panels evaluate how well you understand the fundamentals behind your project. Clearly explain why you chose your specific methodology over alternatives, and be prepared to demonstrate live working prototypes during your demo.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What if my prototype fails during the live final evaluation? Always record clear, high-definition demonstration backup videos showing your working prototype in the lab prior to viva day. If live hardware glitches occur, present your recorded evidence and explain troubleshooting steps.
Can a PSM project be patented or converted into a startup? Yes! Consult the UTeM Centre for Research & Innovation Management (CRIM) for intellectual property (IP) filing and commercialization support before publishing your full code publicly.
