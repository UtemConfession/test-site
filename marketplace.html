🛡️ Safe Trading & Anti-Scam Best Practices

  1. Meet in Well-Lit Public Areas: Conduct all Cash-on-Delivery (COD) handovers in visible campus spots (e.g. Kolej Kediaman Satria Cafeteria, Lestari Lounge, FTMK Foyer, or PLH Library Entrance).
  2. Inspect Before Payment: Thoroughly test electronics, computer peripherals, and appliances on the spot before completing DuitNow QR or cash payment.
  3. Avoid Advance Deposits to Strangers: Never transfer full payments upfront to unverified social media profiles without buyer protection.

Campus Marketplace Safety Guidelines

The UTeM Marketplace is a great place to buy and sell used textbooks, electronics, and offer services. However, always prioritize your safety when conducting transactions.

  • Meet in Public: Always arrange meetups in busy, well-lit areas like the library foyer or residential college cafes during daylight hours.
  • Beware of Scams: If a deal seems too good to be true, it probably is. Never transfer money as a "booking fee" before inspecting the item in person.
  • Verify Students: Prefer trading with current UTeM students. You can ask to see their student ID for verification on high-value items.
