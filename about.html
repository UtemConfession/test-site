About Us

UTeM Confessions Pro Max (UCPM) is an independent, student-operated digital ecosystem built to serve the undergraduate, diploma, and postgraduate communities across all campuses of Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). What began as a student community forum has evolved into a comprehensive digital utility suite designed to simplify campus life, accelerate academic achievement, and foster positive peer discussions.

1. Our Core Mission & Values

Navigating university life can be challenging—from calculating complex credit hour weightages and figuring out shuttle bus timetables to preparing for Final Year Projects (FYP) and hunting for off-campus house rentals. Our mission is to bridge these information gaps by providing accurate, practical, and accessible tools created by students, for students.

2. What We Provide

🎓 Academic Calculators

Dynamic UTeM GPA & CGPA forecasting tools aligned with the university's 4.00 grading scheme, helping students track Dean's List eligibility and credit requirements.

🚌 Campus Transit & Bus Schedules

Real-time route maps and departure timings connecting Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal), Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh), and student residential colleges.

📚 Comprehensive Student Guides

In-depth guides on Industrial Training (LI), FYP survival, PTPTN disbursement & first-class waivers, Kolej Kediaman merit points, and student budgeting.

🛡️ Safe & Moderated Confessions

A secure, anonymous peer confession space protected by anti-spam throttles and strict community guidelines to prevent harassment and cyberbullying.

3. Editorial Standards & Community Moderation

We hold a steadfast commitment to maintaining a positive, helpful, and safe environment. All submissions and articles adhere to our transparent Confession Rules and Privacy Policy. Posts containing hate speech, personal attacks, unverified allegations, or NSFW materials are strictly rejected by our moderation protocols.

4. Independence & Institutional Disclaimer

UTeM Confessions Pro Max is an independent student initiative operated voluntarily by university alumni and current undergraduates. This platform is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) administration. For official university matters, students are encouraged to consult official university portals including SMPWeb, UTeM Portal, and faculty administrative divisions.

5. Contact & Feedback

We welcome suggestions, content corrections, and inquiries from students, lecturers, and partners. Connect with our team via our official Telegram channel at @utemconf or email us at utemconfessionpromax@duck.com.
