UTeM Freshman Survival Guide: Library, Health Center & Wi-Fi Setup

📖 8 Min Read 🎓 Orientation & Student Resources 📍 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka

Welcome to Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM)! Starting your academic journey as a first-year undergraduate or diploma student can feel overwhelming. Beyond attending lectures and labs, knowing how to navigate vital campus services—such as the central library, medical center, IT network, and student portals—is essential for a seamless university experience.

This survival guide compiles everything you need to know about Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH), Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU), campus Wi-Fi connectivity, and essential digital portals.

1. Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) - Your Academic Hub

Located at the heart of Kampus Induk in Durian Tunggal, Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) is UTeM's flagship academic library housing engineering manuals, computer science journals, quiet zones, and discussion rooms.

2. Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU) - Healthcare Services

Pusat Kesihatan Universiti (PKU) provides primary medical, dental, and emergency care to all registered UTeM students.

Service Category Operational Details Location & Access
General Outpatient Clinic Mon - Fri: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Consultations & Medicine) Main PKU Building, Kampus Induk
Dental Clinic (Klinik Pergigian) By Appointment (Scaling, Fillings, Checks) PKU Dental Wing
24/7 Emergency Medical Response On-call medical officer & ambulance standby Emergency Hotline via Auxiliary Police

3. Connecting to UTeM Wi-Fi & Eduroam Network

UTeM provides high-speed wireless internet access across all faculty buildings, hostel blocks, and library halls.

Eduroam Setup Guide:
  • SSID: eduroam
  • EAP Method: PEAP
  • Phase 2 Authentication: MSCHAPv2
  • Identity: student_id@student.utem.edu.my
