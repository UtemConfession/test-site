UTeM Student Resources & Guides Hub

UTeM Confessions Pro Max is an unofficial student-operated digital hub dedicated to serving the undergraduate and postgraduate communities at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). We provide essential tools, academic calculators, campus transit information, and comprehensive student guides:

• UTeM GPA Calculator & Scale Calculate semester GPA, target Dean's List (Anugerah Dekan), and understand probation thresholds. • Shuttle Bus Routes & Schedules Navigate shuttle loops between Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal), Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh), and hostels. • Freshman Onboarding Guide Access Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH), PKU medical clinic services, and Eduroam Wi-Fi setup. • Past Year Exam Strategies Revision strategies for FTMK, FKE, FKM, and FPTT faculties plus official examination hall rules.
Author / Editorial Transparency: Maintained by senior UTeM engineering & computing undergraduates. All guides verified against official UTeM Academic Regulations Handbooks.
