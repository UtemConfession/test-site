UTeM Student Marketplace & Campus Services Directory Guide

UTeM Confessions Pro Max Marketplace & Services is an independent peer-to-peer commerce and service directory connecting diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate students across Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) and Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh). Whether buying pre-loved mechanical engineering textbooks, renting hostel appliances, or engaging trusted student runners, this hub facilitates affordable, sustainable student commerce.

📦 Student Commerce Categories

  • Academic Gear & Textbooks: Pre-owned coursebooks, scientific calculators (Casio 570MS/EX), lab coats, safety boots, and mechanical keyboards.
  • Hostel & Living Essentials: Mini-refrigerators, foldable study desks, induction cookers, electric kettles, and drying racks for Satria & Lestari residential colleges.
  • Student Runner & Personal Shopper (PS): Grocery delivery from Lotus's Cheng, Mydin MITC, and late-night food runs to Durian Tunggal eateries.
  • Academic Services: Fast-turnaround report printing, hardcover thesis binding, 3D printing (PLA/ABS), and PCB soldering assistance.

🛡️ Safe Trading & Anti-Scam Best Practices

  1. Meet in Well-Lit Public Areas: Conduct all Cash-on-Delivery (COD) handovers in visible campus spots (e.g. Kolej Kediaman Satria Cafeteria, Lestari Lounge, FTMK Foyer, or PLH Library Entrance).
  2. Inspect Before Payment: Thoroughly test electronics, computer peripherals, and appliances on the spot before completing DuitNow QR or cash payment.
  3. Avoid Advance Deposits to Strangers: Never transfer full payments upfront to unverified social media profiles without buyer protection.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I list an item for sale or offer student services on this marketplace? Send your item details, price, condition, photos, and Telegram/WhatsApp contact handle to our editorial moderation inbox at utemconfessionpromax@duck.com.
Where are the safest recommended meetup spots for item COD on campus? Always conduct transactions in well-lit public campus areas such as the Kolej Kediaman Satria cafeteria, Lestari student lounge, PLH Library lobby, or faculty foyers.
Are there any listing fees charged for student sellers? No, listing pre-loved academic items, textbooks, hostel gear, and student freelance services is 100% free for all active UTeM undergraduates.
