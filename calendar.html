UTeM Academic Calendar Guide & Session Structure

The UTeM Academic Calendar outlines all official lecture periods, mid-semester breaks, revision weeks, final examination windows, and recognized national/state public holidays for Session 2026/2027 across all faculties including FTMK, FKE, FKM, FKP, FPTT, and FTKEE. Mastering semester timelines helps undergraduates stay ahead of course registration deadlines, project submissions, and examination preparation.

📅 Standard UTeM Semester Architecture

14 Lecture Weeks Split into Phase 1 (Weeks 1–7) and Phase 2 (Weeks 8–14).
1 Mid-Sem Break 1-week recess between Phase 1 and Phase 2 lectures.
1 Revision Week Minggu Ulangkaji for past paper reviews before finals.
3 Exam Weeks Official final written & practical examination sessions.

⏰ Critical Academic Milestones & Deadlines

  • Weeks 1 – 2 (Course Add/Drop Window): Students can add, drop, or switch tutorial/lecture groups via SMPWeb without academic penalty.
  • Weeks 3 – 8 (Course Withdrawal / Tarik Diri): Dropping a subject after Week 2 incurs a 'TD' status on transcripts and requires Dean approval.
  • Week 11 – 12 (Exam Barring List & Timetable Release): Students with attendance below 80% may be barred from sitting final examinations.
  • Week 18+ (Official Result Release): Academic results and CGPA updates published on SMPWeb approximately 2–3 weeks post-exams.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When are UTeM official examination results released on SMPWeb? Official semester examination results are generally released via the UTeM SMPWeb student portal approximately 2 to 3 weeks following the conclusion of final examination week.
What is the duration of a regular UTeM academic semester? A standard UTeM academic semester spans 14 weeks of formal lectures, 1 mid-semester break (1 week), 1 study/revision week (1 week), and 2 to 3 weeks of final examinations.
What is the difference between Semester Khas (Short Sem) and regular semesters? Semester Khas is an intensive 7 to 8-week optional summer term primarily intended for students to repeat failed courses or undertake Industrial Training (Latihan Industri).

Navigating the Academic Calendar

The UTeM Academic Calendar dictates the rhythm of your university life. Keeping track of key milestones is essential for academic success.

  • Course Add/Drop Period: Usually the first two weeks of the semester. This is your only window to adjust your timetable without penalty.
  • Mid-Semester Break: A one-week break typically occurring around Week 7 or 8. Use this time to catch up on assignments and rest.
  • Study Week & Final Exams: The intense period at the end of the 14-week semester. Ensure you print your exam slip from SMP early.
