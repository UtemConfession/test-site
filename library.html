UTeM Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) Student Services Master Guide

Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) serves as the central research and learning knowledge hub for Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka. Operating multi-storey facilities across Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) and a specialized branch at Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh), PLH provides undergraduates with collaborative research spaces, digital archives, and student leisure facilities.

🏢 Available Library Facilities & Zones

  • 24-Hour Study Zone: Dedicated air-conditioned study hall at Ground Floor with secure matric-card access during revision and exam weeks.
  • Bilik Perbincangan (Discussion Rooms): Private acoustic-paneled group project rooms equipped with whiteboards and HDMI presentation monitors.
  • PS4 Gaming & Board Game Zone: Recreational student lounge on Level 1 featuring console gaming and strategy board games for stress relief.
  • Kubikel Individu (Silent Carrels): Distraction-free single-occupancy study pods located across Level 2 and Level 3 stacks.

🔍 Scholarly E-Resources & Database Subscriptions

UTeM students have unlimited institutional access to premier engineering and technology databases including IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Scopus, SpringerLink, Web of Science, and Emerald Insight. Access databases on-campus via campus Wi-Fi or remotely through the UTeM LibProxy gateway.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I book a private discussion room or PS4 console at PLH? Discussion rooms and PS4 console sessions can be reserved in person at the PLH Customer Service Counter (Level 1) or online via the UTeM Library Portal using your active Student ID.
Can I access the 24-hour study area after regular library operating hours? Yes, the 24-Hour Study Zone located at the Ground Floor of PLH Kampus Induk remains accessible via student matric card tap-in even after main counter hours, particularly during exam revision weeks.
How can I renew borrowed library books online? Log into your student account on the UTeM Library OPAC catalog portal (library.utem.edu.my) to renew unreserved books up to two consecutive times before the due date.

Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) Resources

Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) is UTeM's primary academic library, offering a wealth of resources beyond just books.

  • Borrowing Quotas: Undergraduates can typically borrow up to 10 books for 14 days. Postgraduates have higher limits.
  • 24-Hour Study Area: Located on the Ground Floor, accessible 24/7 using your student matrix card. Ideal for exam week revision.
  • PS4 Console & Board Games: Take a break! You can rent the PS4 console or board games at the circulation counter by presenting your student ID.
