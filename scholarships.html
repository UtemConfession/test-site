Scholarship & Financial Aid

Discover government loans, MARA schemes, state foundation grants, corporate awards, and university financial assistance available for UTeM and Malaysian university students.

UTeM Internal Welfare

UTeM Zakat & Emergency Student Welfare Fund (Tabung Kebajikan Pelajar UTeM)

✦ Open All Year Round

Official emergency financial aid provided by UTeM Pusat Islam and Pejabat Hal Ehwal Pelajar (HEP) for B40 undergraduates facing sudden financial hardship, medical emergencies, or living expense distress.

Eligible: All UTeM Undergraduates Facing Emergency Need UTeM Pusat Islam Portal ➔
MARA Sponsorship

MARA TESP & Young Talent Programme (YTP)

✦ Cycle Deadlines: March & August

Convertible loan & sponsorship scheme for Bumiputera students pursuing Diploma, Degree, and Professional studies at IPTA/IPTS in Malaysia including UTeM engineering & IT faculties.

Target: Full-time Diploma / Bachelor Students Official MARA Portal ➔
National Education Loan

PTPTN Higher Education Loan (Pinjaman PTPTN)

✦ Deadlines: April 30 & Sept 30

Primary tertiary education loan scheme for Malaysian undergraduates. First-Class Honours graduates from UTeM may qualify for complete loan-to-scholarship conversion (bebas bayaran balik).

Target: All Malaysian Undergraduates MyPTPTN Application Portal ➔

State Foundation

Biasiswa & Pinjaman Yayasan Pelajaran Johor (YPJ)

✦ Deadlines: Open Quarterly

Maklumat tajaan dan permohonan boleh diakses melalui Portal Rasmi Yayasan Pelajaran Johor (YPJ). Mendaftar dan memohon Pinjaman Pelajaran Dalam Negara atau bantuan di Portal Bantuan YPJ atau sistem pengurusan YPJ Prolims.

Target: Anak Negeri Johor / Permastautin
Portal Rasmi YPJ ➔
State Foundation

Biasiswa Kerajaan Negeri Kedah & iPINJAM

✦ Deadlines: Open Quarterly

Info mengenai Biasiswa Kerajaan Negeri Kedah diuruskan oleh Seksyen Pembiayaan Pendidikan Negeri Kedah (SPPNK). Bagi Derma Siswa atau bantuan pendidikan Zakat, rujuk Lembaga Zakat Negeri Kedah.

Target: Anak Jati / Penduduk Kedah
Portal Zakat Kedah ➔
