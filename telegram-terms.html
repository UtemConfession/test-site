Telegram Terms of Service & Channel Safety Policy

Essential compliance rules, platform safety guidelines, Google service integrations, and mandatory prohibitions designed to protect the 10,000+ student UTeM Confessions channel from permanent Telegram suspension.

1. The Channel Protection Mandate (Why Ban Prevention Matters)

Our primary Telegram broadcast channel (@utemconf) serves as an indispensable daily communication bridge, academic directory, and peer-to-peer student forum for Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka. Under Telegram FZ-LLC's automated terms enforcement, channels that accumulate mass user reports, policy violations, or statutory legal complaints are subject to instant, permanent termination without right of appeal.

To safeguard this public community asset, all submissions submitted through this portal undergo algorithmic and volunteer student moderation before being published to Telegram. If your submission is modified or rejected, it is not personal—it is done to prevent the entire community from losing its voice.

2. ZERO TOLERANCE: Dangerous Drugs & Controlled Substances

UTeM Confessions Pro Max and Telegram strictly prohibit any content related to illicit narcotics, psychotropic substances, or unregulated drug distribution. Malaysia enforces the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 (Act 234), carrying severe criminal penalties including life imprisonment and capital punishment.

✕
Narcotics & Recreational Drugs: Any mention, offer, inquiry, sourcing request, or promotion of marijuana/cannabis (weed), ketum leaves/juice, methamphetamine (syabu/ice), MDMA/ecstasy, ketamine, heroin, or cocaine is immediately rejected and reported to law enforcement where necessary.
✕
Adulterated & "Magic" Vape Pods: Vaping liquids containing illicit adulterants such as psilocybin (magic mushroom pods), synthetic cannabinoids (K2/Spice), DMT, or dangerous chemical additives are strictly classified as illegal substances.
✕
Unregulated Prescription Medication: Reselling or seeking prescription-only pharmaceuticals (e.g., benzodiazepines, Xanax, sleeping pills, stimulant ADHD medications, codeine syrups) without authorized medical licenses violates the Poisons Act 1952 (Act 366).
✕
Campus Disciplinary Sanctions: Pursuant to the Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (Discipline of Students) Rules, drug-related offenses on or off campus result in immediate disciplinary suspension, hostel expulsion, and permanent academic termination.
3. Activities That Cause Instant Channel Bans on Telegram

Telegram's Trust & Safety algorithms scan channels for specific triggers that lead to automatic quarantines, shadowbans, or deletion:

✕
Mass-Reportable Doxxing & Personal Data: Publishing identifiable personal data (IC numbers, matric numbers, personal phone numbers, hostel room numbers, or unconsented photos) triggers coordinated user report campaigns. When multiple users tap "Report ➔ Personal Details", Telegram's AI instantly restricts the channel.
✕
DMCA & Copyright Infringement: Distributing pirated electronic textbooks, paywalled academic test-banks, cracked engineering software (MATLAB, SolidWorks, AutoCAD), or official confidential UTeM exam papers triggers DMCA takedown requests. Telegram strictly complies with rights-holder copyright strikes by disabling entire public channels.
✕
Spam Links, Financial Scams & Bot Radars: Submitting URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), affiliate links, "work-from-home" job schemes, cryptocurrency promotions, online gambling, or unauthorized bot invite links triggers Telegram's automated anti-spam heuristic radar.
✕
Non-Consensual Media & NSFW Imagery: Explicit adult content, screenshots of private chats showing visible phone numbers or profile pictures, and unconsented photography taken in student hostels or private spaces violate Section 233 of the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 (CMA 1998).
4. Google Services Integration & Privacy Architecture

Our platform integrates Google Identity Services (GIS) and Google Workspace utilities to protect both student privacy and channel stability:

✦
Google Sign-In Anonymity Guarantee: Google Sign-In is employed strictly for human verification and anti-spam rate limiting. Your real name, Google profile picture, and email address are processed locally in your browser and are NEVER transmitted to Telegram or published with your confession.
✦
Google Drive Sharing Hygiene: When sharing academic study notes or revision materials via Google Drive, verify that file sharing permissions are set to "Anyone with the link can view". Files must never contain executable programs (.exe, .bat, .apk), password-locked archives, or personal contact info of lecturers.
✦
Institutional Account Caution: We advise against using your official institutional @student.utem.edu.my Google account to host publicly shared files containing controversial opinions or third-party materials, as university Google Workspace domains maintain comprehensive administrative audit logs.
5. Notice, Takedown & Redaction Protocol

If an approved confession post on our Telegram channel inadvertently infringes upon your personal privacy, contains incorrect assertions, or violates platform policies, you have the right to request an immediate, expedited takedown.

Contact our moderation team at utemconfessionpromax@duck.com or reach out directly to our admin bot via Telegram (@utemconf). Provide the specific post number or link and a brief explanation. Legitimate takedown requests are processed and permanently deleted from the channel within 24 hours.
