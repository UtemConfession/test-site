UTeM Kolej Kediaman (Hostel) Merit System & Application Guide

🕒 9 Min Read 🏠 Residential Colleges & Merits 🎓 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
Peer-Reviewed & Fact-Checked Guide Authored by UTeM Senior Undergraduates & verified against official university academic handbooks.
Fact-Checked: August 2026 • Editorial Standards ➔

Securing accommodation in Kolej Kediaman (Residential Colleges) at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) is guaranteed for first-year freshmen. However, for senior undergraduates (Year 2, 3, and 4), on-campus hostel placement is allocated based on the university's Student Merit Point System (Sistem Merit Aktiviti Pelajar).

Living on campus offers unmatched convenience—immediate access to lecture halls, laboratories, dining halls, sports complexes, and internal UTeM Shuttle Buses. This guide breaks down each residential college, how to collect maximum merit points, and the room application timeline.

1. Overview of UTeM Residential Colleges

Residential College Campus Location Room Types & Highlights
Kolej Kediaman Satria Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) Apartment / Quad-sharing & Double-sharing units. Closest to Sports Complex, cafeteria hub, and student activity center.
Kolej Kediaman Lestari Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) Modern high-capacity blocks for undergraduate men and women. Walking distance to central academic complex.
Kolej Kediaman Hang Tuah Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh) Dedicated blocks for engineering technology students stationed at the Technology Campus.

2. How the UTeM Merit System Works

Merit points are earned by actively engaging in extra-curricular activities, leadership roles, sports, and community initiatives registered under the Pusat Sukan, Hal Ehwal Pelajar (HEP), or college committees (JTK). Points are categorized across tiers:

  • International & National Level (40–60 Points): Representing UTeM in MASUM, SUKIPT, Robocon, hackathons, or national innovation expos.
  • University Level (20–35 Points): Jawatankuasa Perwakilan Kolej (JPK), Majlis Perwakilan Pelajar (MPP), or organizing university-wide festivals.
  • Faculty & Club Level (10–20 Points): Committee members in academic clubs (e.g., FTMK Tech Society, Mechanical Engineering Club).
  • College & Activity Participant (5–10 Points): Attending gotong-royong, talks, inter-block sports tournaments, and college spiritual programs.

3. Application Timeline & Process

  1. Semester Check: Verify your cumulative merit points on the UTeM e-Merit / Student Portal around Week 10–12 of each semester.
  2. Online Application: Submit your room preference through the official hostel portal when HEP announces the registration window.
  3. Status Announcement: Results are published before the semester break ends. Successful students download their offer letter (Surat Tawaran Kolej).
  4. Hostel Appeal (Rayuan Kolej): Students who narrowly miss the merit cut-off can submit an online appeal citing active club roles or financial assistance needs (B40 category).

4. Essential Hostel Rules & Living Tips

  • Curfew Hours: Standard campus gate curfew is 11:00 PM for undergraduates. Students returning late due to lab experiments or library study should carry their student matric cards.
  • Electrical Appliances: High-wattage cooking appliances (induction cookers, electric kettles) require electrical permit stickers to prevent tripping block fuses.
  • Room Inspections: Residential staff conduct periodic safety and cleanliness checks. Maintaining high hygiene standards ensures deposit refunds at semester checkout.
💡 Senior Advice: Don't wait until Year 3 to start collecting merits! Join 2–3 active clubs or your college sports team during your first year to build a comfortable merit cushion for senior years.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the minimum merit score needed to secure a hostel room? Cut-off points vary each semester based on bed supply and demand, but a cumulative score of 50+ merit points usually gives you a strong chance.
What if I am not offered a hostel room? You can easily look for rental houses nearby. Check out our UTeM Off-Campus House Rental Guide for top neighborhoods and rental costs.
