Library Hours

  • Academic Semester Hours: Mon - Thu: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
    Friday: 8:00 AM - 12:15 PM, 2:45 PM - 10:00 PM
  • Study & Exam Weeks (Weekends): Sat - Sun: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Selected areas)
  • Semester Break Hours: Mon - Fri: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Closed on Weekends)

PLH Gaming Zone & Leisure

  • PS4 Console & Controller Rental: RM4 per hour (max 2 hours, 2 controllers). 3 sets at Main Campus & 2 sets at Tech Campus. You can bring your own CDs but need to let the staff know.
  • Free Indoor & Board Games: Free rental for carrom, chess, darts, checkers, and board games at the counter.

UTeM Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) Student Guide

Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) is the central academic library system at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka serving Kampus Induk and Kampus Teknologi. Students have access to quiet study zones, 24-hour examination study areas, PS4 gaming consoles, board game rentals, and digital e-resources.
