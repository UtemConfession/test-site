Support UTeM Student Portal, Open Utilities & Community Services

UTeM Confessions Pro Max is an unofficial student-operated digital platform serving undergraduates and postgraduates across all campuses of Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). We maintain free digital tools including the semester GPA / CGPA Calculator, live Bus Schedule Countdown Timetables, Past Year Examination Repository, Pusat Kesihatan (PKU) Emergency Directory, and Perpustakaan Laman Hikmah (PLH) Guides. You can support our ongoing server hosting, domain maintenance, and student software development by tipping via Ko-fi or exploring sponsored student partner offers.
