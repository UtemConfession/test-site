UTeM Shuttle Bus & BAS.MY M10A Transit Guide

The UTeM Shuttle Bus Service connects Kampus Induk (KI / Durian Tunggal), Kampus Teknologi (KT / Ayer Keroh), Emerald Park (EP), Kolej Kediaman Satria, and Kolej Kediaman Lestari. In addition, the public BAS.MY M10A Pink Bus offers weekend direct transit from Melaka Sentral to Zoo Melaka and UTeM Main Campus.
