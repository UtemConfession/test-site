ACADEMIC & GRADING SYSTEM

The Ultimate UTeM GPA & CGPA Calculation Guide

?? 7 Min Read ?? Updated for Academic Year 2026/2027 ?? Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka

Maintaining a strong academic standing at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) requires a clear understanding of how Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) are evaluated across faculties including FTMK, FKE, FKM, FKTEN, FPTT, and FTKEE.

Whether you are a diploma or degree student striving for the Dean's List (Anugerah Dekan) or seeking to recover your CGPA after a tough semester, this guide outlines the official grade values, mathematical formulas, academic status thresholds, and strategy tips.

1. Understanding Grade Point Values at UTeM

Each course at UTeM is assigned a specific number of credit hours (typically 2 to 4 credits per subject). At the end of every semester, your raw percentage mark translates into a letter grade and a corresponding Grade Point Value (GPV):

Percentage Marks Letter Grade Grade Point Value (GPV) Academic Performance Status
80% - 100% A 4.00 Cemerlang (Excellent)
75% - 79% A- 3.67 Sangat Baik (Very Good)
70% - 74% B+ 3.33 Kepujian Tinggi (High Honors)
65% - 69% B 3.00 Kepujian (Honors)
60% - 64% B- 2.67 Lulus (Pass)
55% - 59% C+ 2.33 Lulus (Pass)
50% - 54% C 2.00 Lulus (Satisfactory Pass)
45% - 49% C- 1.67 Lulus Bersyarat (Conditional Pass)
40% - 44% D+ 1.33 Lulus Bersyarat (Conditional Pass)
35% - 39% D 1.00 Lulus Bersyarat (Conditional Pass)
0% - 34% E / F 0.00 Gagal (Fail - Must Repeat)

2. The Mathematical Formulas for GPA & CGPA

Grade Point Average (GPA)

GPA measures your academic performance during a single specific semester. It is calculated by multiplying the Grade Point Value of each subject by its credit hours, summing the products, and dividing by the total registered credit hours for that semester:

GPA Formula:

GPA = S (Subject Credit Hours × Grade Point Value) / Total Semester Credit Hours

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

CGPA tracks your cumulative academic trajectory across all completed semesters from your first day at UTeM until graduation:

CGPA Formula:

CGPA = Total Quality Points Earned Across All Semesters / Total Cumulative Credit Hours

3. UTeM Dean's List (Anugerah Dekan) Requirements

To qualify for the prestigious Anugerah Dekan at UTeM, undergraduate students must fulfill all of the following official criteria during the active semester:

  • Achieve a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher.
  • Be registered for a minimum of 12 credit hours in that semester (excluding short semester unless specified).
  • Have no failing grades (E/F) or incomplete grades (TL) in any registered subject.
  • Have not been subjected to any disciplinary actions under UTeM student conduct rules.

4. Academic Standing & Status Definitions

UTeM evaluates student academic standing at the conclusion of every semester based on CGPA thresholds:

  • Kedudukan Baik (KB) - Good Standing: Awarded when a student maintains a CGPA of 2.00 or higher.
  • Kedudukan Bersyarat (KS) - Probationary Standing: Issued when a student's CGPA drops between 1.70 and 1.99. Restricted in maximum credit hours next semester.
  • Diberhentikan (D) - Academic Dismissal: Occurs if a student remains in KS for two consecutive semesters, or if CGPA drops below 1.70.
