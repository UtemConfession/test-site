UTeM Off-Campus House Rental Guide: Durian Tunggal & Ayer Keroh

🕒 8 Min Read 🏡 Neighborhoods & Tenancy 🎓 Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
Peer-Reviewed & Fact-Checked Guide Authored by UTeM Senior Undergraduates & verified against official university academic handbooks.
Fact-Checked: August 2026 • Editorial Standards ➔

Renting a house off-campus (Rumah Sewa Luar) is a rite of passage for many Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) undergraduates after their first year. While living outside offers independence, private cooking facilities, and flexible study hours, selecting the right location and drafting a secure tenancy agreement is crucial to avoid disputes.

This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown of top residential neighborhoods near Kampus Induk (Durian Tunggal) and Kampus Teknologi (Ayer Keroh), current market rental rates, and essential tips for managing deposits and utilities.

2. Monthly Utility & Living Expenses Breakdown

When calculating your off-campus monthly budget, account for recurring overheads beyond basic rent:

  • Electricity (TNB): Approx. RM20–RM50 per person monthly (higher if bedrooms feature air-conditioning).
  • Water (SAMB): Approx. RM5–RM10 per person monthly.
  • Home Wi-Fi (TIME / Unifi): Approx. RM15–RM25 per person when split among 5–7 housemates for a 100Mbps/300Mbps plan.
  • Daily Commute Fuel: RM30–RM80 monthly depending on whether you commute via motorcycle or car. Don't forget to obtain an official UTeM Vehicle Sticker for campus parking.

3. Tenancy Agreement Checklist & Deposit Safety

Before transferring any booking deposit to a landlord, protect yourself with this checklist:

  1. Physical House Viewing (Lawatan Rumah): Never pay a deposit based solely on social media photos. Inspect water pressure, ceiling leakages, door locks, and electrical outlets in person.
  2. Standard Deposit Structure: Standard practice in Melaka is 1+1 or 2+1 (1-2 months security deposit + 1 month advance rental + half month utility deposit). Request an itemized physical or digital receipt signed by the owner.
  3. Tenancy Contract (Surat Perjanjian Sewa Rumah): Ensure the contract clearly defines responsibilities for major plumbing/structural repairs vs minor maintenance (e.g., lightbulbs, minor wear and tear).
  4. Inventory Record: Take time-stamped photos and videos of existing furniture and wall condition on move-in day to ensure full deposit recovery upon checkout.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is public transit available from rental areas to UTeM? Dedicated UTeM shuttle buses service selected student residential areas during peak morning and evening lecture hours. Check our Bus Transit Guide for route timetables.
